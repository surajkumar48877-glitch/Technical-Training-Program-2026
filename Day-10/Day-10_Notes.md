# Day 10 – Python Notes

## 🔹 Finding Duplicate Values and Their Count

We can use a dictionary to store the values which occur more than one time.

Example:

```python
my_list = [5, 7, 2, 3, 7, 8, 2, 3, 3]
```

Values occurring more than once:

```text
7 → 2 times
2 → 2 times
3 → 3 times
```

Output:

```text
{7: 2, 2: 2, 3: 3}
```

## 🔹 Separate Even and Odd Numbers

Given a list of numbers, separate:

* Even numbers → divisible by 2
* Odd numbers → not divisible by 2

Example:

```text
10 98 3 33 12 22 21 11
```

Output:

```text
10 98 12 22 3 33 21 11
```

First all even numbers are placed, then all odd numbers.

## 🔹 Maximum Product of Two Values

We have to find the maximum product of two values from a list.

Example:

```text
7 9 -3 8 -6 -7 8 10
```

The maximum product is:

```text
9 × 10 = 90
```

So the output is:

```text
90
```

## 🔹 Finding Perfect Square Values

Given a list of numbers, find which values are perfect squares.

Example:

```text
79 77 54 81 48 34 25 16
```

Perfect squares are:

```text
81 = 9 × 9
25 = 5 × 5
16 = 4 × 4
```

Output:

```text
81
25
16
```
